ClinicOne — Audit Trail & Compliance

Field-level Diff • Tamper-evident Logs • Policies • Retention

End-to-end auditability untuk setiap perubahan data kritis: create, write, unlink, state transition, approval, hingga export. Dirancang untuk Odoo 18 Community Edition dan terintegrasi secara holistik dengan modul ClinicOne lainnya.

Odoo 18 CE Multi-company Mail/Activities Reports Security & Policy

Overview

Modul ini mencatat perubahan data pada level field (before/after), pengguna, waktu, dan konteks tindakan. Tersedia policy engine untuk mewajibkan alasan perubahan, level approval, hingga tamper-evident hash. Log dapat diekspor (CSV/PDF) untuk kebutuhan audit eksternal, serta memiliki retention policy yang dapat dikonfigurasi.

Key Features

  • Field-level diff (old → new) untuk create, write, unlink.
  • Policy & Controls: mandatory reason, approver chain, sensitive field guard.
  • Tamper-evident: hashing berantai (log chain) & signature metadata.
  • Event Hooks: stage/state change, approval, post to mail.activity.
  • Retention & Purge: jadwal otomatis, TTL per kategori data.
  • Export & Evidence: PDF/CSV, nomor bukti, watermark, cap waktu.
  • Review Queue: antrian investigasi & rekomendasi tindakan.

Core Models

  • clinic.audit.log — header log, actor, context, hash chain.
  • clinic.audit.log.line — detail per field (before/after, type, label).
  • clinic.audit.policy — rule builder: model, fields, triggers, actions.
  • clinic.audit.review — review & disposition (investigate/close/escalate).
  • res.config.settings (inherit) — konfigurasi retention & global flags.

Model dibuat generik agar dapat memantau modul lain tanpa hard dependency.

Installation

  1. Tambahkan addon ini ke addons_path Odoo Anda.
  2. Update daftar Apps, cari ClinicOne: Audit Trail & Compliance.
  3. Install; dependensi inti akan terpasang otomatis.

Configuration

  • Atur Global Settings: enable logging, hash chain, retention TTL.
  • Buat Audit Policies: scope model, fields, trigger, action (reason/approval).
  • Opsional: aktifkan mail.activity pada event tertentu.

Security

  • Groups: Audit User, Auditor, Compliance Manager, Admin.
  • Record Rules: pembatasan per company & model yang diizinkan.
  • Data Masking: opsi sembunyikan nilai sensitif pada tampilan log.

Demo & Testing

  • Demo: demo_policy.xml, demo_logs.xml (sandbox only).
  • Unit tests: create/write/unlink, policy enforcement, retention purge.
  • UAT: perubahan tagihan, ubah harga treatment, pembatalan transaksi, approval.

Performance Notes

  • Index: model, res_id, create_date, user_id, company_id, severity.
  • Gunakan policy scope untuk membatasi high-churn models.
  • Arsipkan/rotate log lama sesuai SLA dan regulasi.
