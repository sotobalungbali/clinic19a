ClinicOne — Enterprise Billing

Clinical billing, accounting, payments, benefits, insurance and provider commissions

ClinicOne Billing is the financial orchestration layer for Odoo 19 Community Edition. It preserves clinical traceability from booking, encounter, care plan, package and eMAR through billing documents, accounting invoices, split payments, insurance, vouchers, commissions and downstream integration events.

Designed for multi-company clinics with ORM-enforced lifecycle controls, professional search/list/form views, auditable source links, and Odoo-native accounting integration.
