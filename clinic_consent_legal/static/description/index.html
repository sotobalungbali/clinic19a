ClinicOne — Consent & Legal Forms

Digital Consents • E-Signature • Integrity Hash • Portal Signing

A production-ready module to manage procedure consents and legal forms with portal signing, guardian/representative support, and audit-grade integrity. Seamlessly integrated across the ClinicOne suite on Odoo 18 Community Edition.

Odoo 18 CE Multi-company Mail/Activities Portal Security

Overview

Centralizes legal templates, patient consent records, and e-signatures with clear lifecycle: Draft → Waiting for Signature → Signed → Archived. Includes validity & expiry, reminders, content versioning, and content integrity hashing for audit readiness.

Key Features

  • Template-driven content (HTML/Text) with version marker
  • E-Signature capture (name, image, timestamp, IP, user-agent)
  • Integrity hash (SHA-256) over key legal fields at sign time
  • Guardian/Representative signing & relationship tracking
  • Required-before-procedure policy with workflow locks
  • Validity (days), computed expiry date & expiry checks
  • Portal links for patient review & signing
  • Reminders via activities / mail templates
  • Attachments and chatter for full audit trail

Core Models

  • clinic.consent.form — primary legal record & signature lifecycle
  • clinic.consent.template — reusable legal content & defaults
  • clinic.consent.signature — signature meta (optional split model)
  • clinic.consent.attachment — supporting documents registry

Inherits mail/thread & portal mixins for activities, followers, and patient portal access.

Installation

  1. Add this addon to your Odoo addons_path.
  2. Update Apps list, search ClinicOne: Consent & Legal Forms.
  3. Install; dependencies auto-resolve.

Configuration

  • Define Consent Templates (title, HTML, text, version, validity).
  • Set up mail templates & server actions for reminders.
  • Verify sequence clinic.consent.form is active.
  • Optional: expose portal menu for patient access.

Security

  • Groups: Consent User, Consent Manager, Portal User (Patient)
  • Record Rules: company scope; patients see their own consents
  • Signed/Archived records are protected from edits

Typical Flows

  • Pre-appointment: auto-create consent from booking/treatment
  • Portal signing: patient/guardian signs via secure link
  • In-clinic: staff triggers signature capture on tablet
  • Post-signature: compute integrity hash, set expiry, archive later

Performance Notes

  • Index frequently filtered fields: state, company_id, patient_id, doctor_id, treatment_id, expiry_date
  • Avoid heavy computes in list views; prefer stored fields
  • Archive signed/expired consents periodically in high-volume clinics
