ClinicOne — Inventory & Stock for Aesthetic Clinics

FEFO • Shelf-life Control • Pharmacy & Treatment Rooms • Quarantine • Replenishment

End-to-end inventory tailored for clinical workflows: products, lots/serials, expiry, room-level control, internal transfers, consumption, and replenishment — all integrated across the ClinicOne suite. Compatible with Odoo 18 Community Edition.

Odoo 18 CE Multi-company Mail/Activities Expiry Control Dashboards

Overview

This module extends Odoo stock to meet the needs of aesthetic clinics. It introduces pharmacy and treatment room concepts, FEFO enforcement hints, minimum shelf-life policies, quarantine flows, and clinical consumption tied to treatments — with soft-coupled hooks to patient, doctor, booking, billing, and reporting modules.

Key Features

  • Product extensions for clinical attributes (medical, cosmetic, device, package/bundle)
  • FEFO & shelf-life hints; min remaining shelf-life filter at reservation
  • Warehouse roles: Pharmacy, Treatment Rooms Root, Quarantine
  • Clinical consumption document → auto stock moves (with lots/serials)
  • Doctor-allowed products (allow/deny by product/category)
  • Patient product history (consume/deliver/return/quarantine)
  • Reorder points (scope-aware) with buffer & rounding policy
  • Inventory adjustment with snapshot, governance, and expired routing
  • Portal-ready summaries (optional), dashboards, and printable reports

Core Models

  • product.template/product.product — clinical fields & helpers
  • stock.location — pharmacy/treatment/quarantine flags & policies
  • stock.rule — clinic FEFO/shelf-life & destination policy hints
  • stock.warehouse.orderpoint — scope metrics & suggestions
  • clinic.treatment.product.usage — clinical consumption
  • clinic.patient.product.history — patient-level usage log
  • clinic.doctor.allowed.product — allow/deny matrix
  • clinic.inventory.adjustment — snapshot-driven adjustments
  • procurement.group (extended) — clinical context propagation

Hooks & events: integration service enables soft-coupled behaviors across the ClinicOne ecosystem.

Installation

  1. Add this addon to your Odoo addons_path.
  2. Update Apps list, search ClinicOne: Inventory & Stock.
  3. Install; dependencies will auto-resolve.

Configuration

  • Settings → ClinicOne → Inventory: define FEFO, min shelf-life, scope, expiration policy
  • On Warehouse: set Pharmacy, Treatment Rooms Root, and Quarantine locations
  • Define Reorder Points; tune buffer & rounding
  • Optionally enable doctor allowlist and patient history

Security

  • Groups: Inventory User, Inventory Manager, Pharmacy Manager, Clinic Admin
  • Record Rules: company scope, treatment room visibility, quarantine controls

Demo & Testing

  • Demo data: product_demo.xml, lot_batch_demo.xml, stock_quant_demo.xml
  • Unit tests: product models, reservations, wizards, security, integration flows
  • UAT scenarios: FEFO conflict, expiring lots, treatment consumption, quarantine routing

Performance Notes

  • Index heavily filtered fields: company_id, product_id, location_id, lot_id, state, date
  • Use domains in dashboards; avoid heavy computes in list views
  • Archive historical movements periodically in high-volume environments

Reports

  • Inventory Balance, Stock Card, Batch Expiry, Treatment Consumption

API Endpoints

  • controllers/portal_inventory.py — limited portal views (optional)
  • controllers/webhook.py — inbound webhooks for scanners or IoT

Upgrade & Migration
