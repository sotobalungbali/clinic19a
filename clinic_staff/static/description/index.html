ClinicOne — Clinical Staff (Nurse & Therapist) Management

Skill Matrix • Licenses • Shift & Roster • Workload • Assignment • KPI

Kelola tenaga klinis (Perawat & Terapis) secara terpadu: kompetensi/skill-matrix, lisensi & masa berlaku, level/grade, shift & roster, workload, serta penugasan ke ruangan/antrian/tindakan. Terintegrasi dengan Incident, Post-Care Task, Telemedicine, dan KPI dashboard. Kompatibel dengan Odoo 18 Community Edition.

Odoo 18 CE Multi-company Mail/Activities Calendar/Gantt KPI Dashboard

Overview

Modul ini memusatkan pengelolaan Master Staff (kompetensi/skill, lisensi/masa berlaku, grade), Shift & Roster, Workload, dan Assignment ke ruangan/antrian/tindakan. Dilengkapi hak akses terpisah (FO/Therapist/Nurse/Doctor/Manager) dan keterkaitan ke Incident, Post-Care Task, Telemedicine chat, serta KPI & presence.

Key Features

  • Master Staff: profile, role, grade/level, team, branch, skill-matrix, status eligibility
  • Licenses: jenis lisensi, nomor & issuer, masa berlaku, reminder & renewal wizard
  • Shift & Roster: template shift, roster Gantt, swap shift, conflict validation
  • Workload: target vs actual (hour/encounter/procedure), overtime & workload rebalance
  • Assignment: penugasan ke Room/Queue/Procedure dengan kapasitas & kualifikasi
  • Presence: check-in/out, break, auto-eligibility untuk menerima tugas
  • KPI: utilization, procedures done, revenue allocation, NPS/feedback
  • Integrations: Incident, Post-Care, Telemedicine, Billing/Finance/Accounting/Inventory

Core Models

  • clinic.staff — staff profile, role, grade, eligibility
  • clinic.staff.skill — skill-matrix & validation
  • clinic.staff.license — license & expiry tracking + renewal
  • clinic.staff.availability — availability slots & calendar
  • clinic.staff.roster — shift roster (Gantt)
  • clinic.staff.assignment — assignment ke room/queue/procedure
  • clinic.staff.workload — workload target & actual
  • clinic.staff.kpi — KPI aggregates, scorecards
  • clinic.staff.presence — check-in/out & presence state

Semua model mendukung mail.thread & mail.activity.mixin untuk jejak audit & kolaborasi.

Installation

  1. Tambahkan addon ini ke addons_path Odoo Anda.
  2. Update daftar Apps, cari ClinicOne: Clinical Staff Management.
  3. Install; dependencies akan terpasang otomatis.

Configuration

  • Definisikan Grades dan Skills sesuai kebutuhan layanan.
  • Masukkan Licenses & set reminder untuk expiry.
  • Siapkan Shift Templates & generate Roster.
  • Atur Access Groups (FO/Therapist/Nurse/Doctor/Manager).

Security

  • Groups: Staff User, Roster Manager, Clinic Manager, Admin.
  • Record Rules: per company/branch & responsibility scope.

Wizards

  • generate_roster_wizard — generate roster dari template & demand
  • assign_staff_wizard — quick assign ke room/queue/procedure
  • swap_shift_wizard — tukar shift dengan conflict validation
  • license_renewal_wizard — perpanjangan lisensi & aktivitas
  • workload_rebalance_wizard — redistribusi workload

Reports & Dashboards

  • Roster Gantt & Availability Calendar
  • License Expiry & Compliance
  • KPI: utilization, procedures done, revenue allocation

Assets

  • JS: staff_dashboard.js
  • QWeb: staff_dashboard_templates.xml
  • SCSS: staff_styles.scss

API Hooks

  • Presence → Eligibility (auto-enable assignment)
  • Billing → Revenue Allocation → Settlement
  • Incident/Post-Care/Telemedicine → Staff linking

Upgrade & Migration

  • Consistent model prefix clinic.* untuk antar-modul.
  • Migration helpers: sequences, analytic mapping, data loaders.
  • Backward-compat field aliases bila diperlukan.
